Welcome to pr1maly

Your fully local CS2 analytics suite. Parse demo files, track performance trends, replay rounds, and get AI-powered insights — all on your machine.

hard_drive Storage

A demo costs roughly 280 MB on disk but about 1.3 MB once analysed, and nothing reads it again afterwards. Keeping the newest ones lets you re-analyse them after changing how metrics are calculated — that is the only reason to hold on to a demo.
